Licensed pros only

In Alabama, applying fertilizer and weed control for hire requires a Horticulture Professional Services license from the Alabama Department of Agriculture & Industries in the Ornamental & Turf Pest Control category, with a certified operator. Only pros with that license appear for this service on MowBrokers.

How the program works

The MowBrokers program is a set number of applications spread across the year, priced per application by lot size. Your pro decides what goes down at each visit for your grass and season, and notes it in the service report.

Bundle with mowing

Are the companies licensed and insured?

Every pro on MowBrokers has a verified city business license and general liability insurance we verify with the carrier, and passes our vetting: background checks on the owner and crew, three customer references and a ride-along on a mowing route. When do I pay?

You save a card when you book and you're charged only after each visit. Season prepay at the first visit is optional and saves a little more.

What if a cut is missed or weeds come back?
