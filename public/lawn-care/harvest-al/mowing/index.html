Weekly service keeps a lawn at an even height through the growing season. Every-two-weeks service costs less over the season, but each cut usually costs a little more because the grass is longer; pros post that premium up front.

Need a single cut before a party, a sale or while you're away? Choose a one-time cut. Pros price it as a multiple of their weekly cut, shown before you book.

Are the companies licensed and insured?

Every pro on MowBrokers has a verified city business license and general liability insurance we verify with the carrier, and passes our vetting: background checks on the owner and crew, three customer references and a ride-along on a mowing route. Pros who apply fertilizer or weed control also hold the ADAI Horticulture Professional Services license in the Ornamental & Turf Pest Control category.

When do I pay?

You save a card when you book and you're charged only after each visit. Season prepay at the first visit is optional and saves a little more.

What if a cut is missed or weeds come back?

If a scheduled cut is missed or done poorly, or weeds come back between treatment visits, your pro comes back within two business days at no charge. Request it from your account page.
