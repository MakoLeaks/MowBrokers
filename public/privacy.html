Privacy Policy

We collect the details you enter (name, phone, email, service address and yard details) to price and book your service, and we share them only with the provider you choose or we assign. Card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. We use Resend to send email and Twilio to send texts you opt into. We do not sell your information.

Providers' business details, licenses, insurance and prices are shown publicly on their profiles. Insurance certificates and internal vetting notes are private. Visit photos are private to you, your provider and the MowBrokers team.
