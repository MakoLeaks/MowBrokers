Licensing in Alabama

Mowing and yard maintenance need no state license: you need a city business license and general liability insurance.

Applying fertilizer, weed control or any pesticide or herbicide for hire requires the Alabama Department of Agriculture & Industries Horticulture Professional Services license in the Ornamental & Turf Pest Control (OTPS) category, with a certified operator. Only pros with OTPS can offer the fertilizer and weed control program on MowBrokers.

Tree and stump removal jobs need the Tree Surgery (TS) category; landscape bed installs need Setting of Landscape Plants (SLP).

How vetting works

  1. We verify your city business license.
  2. If you offer treatments, we confirm your ADAI Horticulture license and OTPS certified operator.
  3. You upload a certificate of insurance; we verify it with your carrier.
  4. Background checks on the owner and crew who visit homes.
  5. We call three current customers.
  6. One ride-along on a regular mowing route.
  7. You sign the provider agreement and connect Stripe for payouts. Your profile goes live.
